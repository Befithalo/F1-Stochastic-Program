How to read this page

Expected finish
The average finishing position across every simulated race. P8.5 means the model expects about eighth or ninth.
Bad-day finish
The average of the worst 1 in 10 simulated races. It shows how far things can go wrong.
Podium chance
The share of simulated races that end in P1, P2 or P3.
Points chance
The share of simulated races that end in the top 10.
9-in-10 range
Nine of every ten simulated races finish inside this range. The dot is the expected finish.
Pit window
The laps where stopping gives about the same result, so the exact lap matters little.
Close call
The best two options are so near that the simulation cannot reliably separate them.
Risk setting
Aggressive chases the best average; cautious protects against a bad day. Balanced weighs both.
