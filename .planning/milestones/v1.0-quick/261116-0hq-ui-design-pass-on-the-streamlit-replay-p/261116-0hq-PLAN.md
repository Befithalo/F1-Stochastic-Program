## Approved problem list (step 2)

1. The page is broken: a started out-lap stint has no completed laps, and `prepare_stint_bar_data`
   crashes on its missing last lap.
2. On phone, chart headings end up above the wrong chart.
3. Fan chart: bands read backwards, and labels are unreadable and in the wrong order.
4. Stint chart: no lap axis or "now" marker; the recommended stint is a grey bar; the selected
   driver is not in team colour.
5. The recommended option shows a red Soft tyre badge.
6. One decision has several names; the lede is repeated; tire/tyre spelling is mixed.
7. Gap chart: noisy; pit spikes set the scale; leader lines look like data; titles are labels,
   not findings.
8. Finish-chances grid: a 0% cell is mid-grey; the colour is teal, not the team colour; no key;
   phone position labels collide.
9. Text only: the mockup's small visuals are missing; help text repeats on every row; one
   one-row table per option.
10. Model report: jargon; numbers shown twice; no comparison bars.
