# Roadmap: F1 Stochastic Intelligence Platform

## Shipped Milestones

### v1.0 — M1 Core Stochastic Engine (shipped 2026-11-17)

Eight phases and 55 plans completed. M1 was administratively closed with an override: 37 of 38 requirements were met. The locked Phase 7 PAC-06 real-data gate was not met and carries into M2 under DEC-130. Seven verification, UAT, and deferred items were acknowledged and are listed in `.planning/milestones/v1.0-MILESTONE-AUDIT.md` and the archived STATE.md.

- Phase 1: Foundation and data access
- Phase 2: Interfaces, linear tire wear, and free-track optimizer
- Phase 3: Monte Carlo simulator
- Phase 4: Kalman tire filter
- Phase 5: Longstaff-Schwartz strategy and live replay
- Phase 6: Ten-race backtest and evidence view
- Phase 7: Bayesian pace model (PAC-06 unmet)
- Phase 8: Markov regimes, 20-race in-sample backtest, and separate 2026 report

The full phase requirements, plans, decisions, reports, and closeout record are archived in `.planning/milestones/v1.0-*`.
