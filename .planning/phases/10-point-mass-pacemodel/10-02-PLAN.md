## Edge-probe predicates implemented here

PHY-02 is explicitly classified as `numeric-range` plus `collection` per DEC-142. The fit accepts only an eligible, ordered, non-empty panel with finite SI float64 fields; it canonicalizes row order by race, lap, and driver. Its inclusive numeric bounds are evaluated by a bounded optimizer, but a boundary optimum is reported as `boundary_limited` and retains the supplied assumption. An empty panel and any failed rank/condition gate produce an explicit unidentifiable report. These predicates complete PHY-02 boundary, empty, ordering, and precision edge coverage.
