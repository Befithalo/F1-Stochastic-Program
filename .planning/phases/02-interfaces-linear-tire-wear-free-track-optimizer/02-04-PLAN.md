## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** see the fastest 1-stop and 2-stop pit plan for a driver, found by checking every legal combination under the fitted tyre model, **so that** I have an honest baseline to compare real team strategies and later risk-aware recommendations against.

**ROADMAP goal (verbatim):** User can get the fastest free-track 1- or 2-stop plan for a race from fitted, fuel-corrected tire wear, behind interfaces later milestones can plug into.

Delivers success criterion 5 and the `StrategyPolicy` conformance half of criterion 2.
