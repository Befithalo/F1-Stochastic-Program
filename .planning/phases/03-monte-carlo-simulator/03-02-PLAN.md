## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** build a `RaceState` that knows whether a safety car is out at the cutoff and how uncertain each driver's pace is, **so that** the simulator starts from the true race situation and samples honest parameter uncertainty.

**ROADMAP goal for Phase 3 (verbatim):** User can simulate 50,000 continuations of a race from any `RaceState` in under 10 seconds with a reliable, low-variance result.

<objective>
Extend the Phase 2 contracts with the plumbing the simulator needs: SC regime and SC pit-loss placeholder in the state builder, the pace-offset standard error, and the diagnostics fields of `SimulationResult`.

Purpose: success criterion 3 needs `pit_loss_s["sc"]`; D-12 needs the offset SE; D-19 needs diagnostics. Doing it first keeps plans 03-03 and 03-04 free of cross-cutting edits.
Output: updated `state.py`, `linear.py`, `interfaces.py`, conformance suite/stubs, tests, DEC-076 and DEC-077.
</objective>
