## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** pass a `RaceState` and get 50,000 simulated finishes for every car, with safety cars, pit stops and passing, **so that** I can see a driver's finishing-position distribution and trust that the same seed gives the same answer.

**ROADMAP goal for Phase 3 (verbatim):** User can simulate 50,000 continuations of a race from any `RaceState` in under 10 seconds with a reliable, low-variance result.

Output: `src/f1si/sim/{plans,borrow,numpy_backend}.py`, seven test modules, registry entry, DEC-081 and DEC-082.
