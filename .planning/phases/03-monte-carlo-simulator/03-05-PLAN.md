## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** know that 50,000 simulated races finish in under 10 seconds and that the variance-reduced sampling is measurably less noisy than plain Monte Carlo, **so that** I can trust the speed and the stability of the numbers the strategy layer will build on.

**ROADMAP goal for Phase 3 (verbatim):** User can simulate 50,000 continuations of a race from any `RaceState` in under 10 seconds with a reliable, low-variance result.
