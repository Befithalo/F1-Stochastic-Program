## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** have a per-lap safety-car probability estimated only from earlier races, with a prior when none exist, **so that** the simulator's safety-car randomness is honest, look-ahead free and verified against a known synthetic rate.

**ROADMAP goal for Phase 3 (verbatim):** User can simulate 50,000 continuations of a race from any `RaceState` in under 10 seconds with a reliable, low-variance result.

<objective>
Install the Phase 3 dependencies, extend the synthetic generator with a safety-car spec, and build the REG-01 safety-car rate estimator with its recovery test and look-ahead registration.

Purpose: REG-01 is a thin, fully testable slice (history in, rate and duration out) that the simulator in later plans consumes; the dependencies are Wave 0 for the whole phase.
Output: scipy/numba/scipy-stubs in pyproject.toml and uv.lock, generator SC spec, `src/f1si/regime/sc_rate.py`, tests, scramble consumer, DEC-074 and DEC-075, the Phase 3 explainer stub.
</objective>
