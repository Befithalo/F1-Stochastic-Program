## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** see each car's hidden tyre-wear rate estimated lap by lap with an honest uncertainty band, **so that** I can trust a calibrated, as-of wear estimate.

**ROADMAP Phase 4 goal:** a Kalman filter estimates each car's hidden tyre-wear rate lap by lap, with a narrowing band, from data known at each lap (PAC-03, PAC-04). Success criterion 2 is restated by the owner (D-17): the filter reaches the best accuracy the data allows; the 10% figure is reported, not gated.

Purpose: the filter becomes usable on a `RaceSnapshot` without look-ahead, and its calibration is verified against synthetic ground truth (project constraint).
Output: generator fields, adapter and priors in `src/f1si/pace/kalman.py`, adapter and recovery tests, guard registration, DEC-091 and DEC-092.
</objective>
