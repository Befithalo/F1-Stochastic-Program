## Phase Goal

**As a** quant-minded F1 strategy analyst, **I want to** have each car's filtered wear rate and its uncertainty feed the race simulator, **so that** finishing-position distributions reflect what is actually known about each car's tyres at the cutoff lap.

**ROADMAP Phase 4 success criterion 4:** the wear mean and SD from the filter populate the `RaceState` fields that the simulator reads.

<objective>
Add `wear_source` to `CarState`, let `build_race_state` take the filter's current-stint posterior, and make the NumPy simulator use a filter car's own rate and SD for its current tyres, without touching the kernel or any existing draw.

Purpose: success criterion 4 (PAC-03 wiring); ties the filter into the simulator while keeping Phase 3 byte-identical for fit cars.
Output: state.py changes, draws helper, numpy_backend virtual compound columns, tests, DEC-093 and DEC-094.
</objective>
